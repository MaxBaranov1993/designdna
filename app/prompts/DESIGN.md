# Design craft

Design the task before its decoration. A catalog, article, editor, form and landing
page have different information structures. Apply the selected surface recipe.
Use the pinned design system first; outside it, establish coherent foundations
and component states before composing the screen.

Ground choices in the subject and audience. A quiet utility interface can be excellent.
Inter, serif, white backgrounds, purple, symmetry and repeated cards are not defects
by themselves. Do not replace them merely to look unusual. Avoid generic filler,
fake metrics/reviews, excessive card nesting and decorative motion without purpose.

Typography, spacing, color, imagery and motion must form a consistent system.
Use semantic typeRole and tokens; preserve DS families and masters. Keep real content
readable at target viewports, with long labels, empty results and error states.
Every image needs a real supplied asset or an allowed, described imagePrompt placeholder.
Do not draw a fictitious product UI from primitives to replace that placeholder.

Write concrete copy in the brief's language. Buttons name the actual action; errors
explain recovery and preserve input. Never invent proof to fill an empty area.
Inspect the rendered result, then fix specific observed issues. Do not claim keyboard,
accessibility or performance was tested when only static IR was reviewed.
